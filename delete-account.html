Delete your account

For Coronet, Branchline and other games by Acloe Technology Ventures

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open the game and tap the settings button (top right).
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete my account, then confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your leaderboard name, your scores and your usage data from our servers, straight away. One account is shared by all our games, so this removes it for every game. Progress saved on your device stays until you uninstall the game.

By request

If you can't open the game, write to the developer email on its Google Play or App Store page with the subject Delete my account and your player name. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What we keep

Nothing. Deleted accounts are not kept in backups for longer than 30 days.
